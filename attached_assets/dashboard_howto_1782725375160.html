How to use AI Recommendations ▼

AI Recommendations adds smart, AI-picked “You may also like” product suggestions to your storefront — on your product pages and inside your cart drawer. No code to paste; you add the components from your theme editor.

1. Start your subscription

  1. Open the app from Apps in your Shopify admin and approve the permissions.
  2. Start the plan (14-day free trial). Recommendations only display while the trial/subscription is active.

2. Add the product-page rail

  1. Go to Online Store → Themes → Customize.
  2. In the top dropdown choose a Products template.
  3. Under the product section click ＋ Add block → choose AI Recommendations (under Apps).
  4. Drag it where you want it and click Save. Shoppers can pick a variant via swatches and add to cart right from the rail.

3. Turn on the cart-drawer carousel

  1. Still in Customize, open App embeds (puzzle-piece icon).
  2. Switch AI Cart Recommendations On and click Save.
  3. It shows a rotating recommendation in the cart drawer, and hides itself when the cart is empty.

4. Custom CSS (optional)

Each component has a Custom CSS box in its theme-editor settings — paste your own CSS to match your brand. Target classes:

Product rail: .boko-reco, .boko-reco__title, .boko-reco__card, .boko-reco__price, .boko-reco__atc
Cart carousel: .boko-cc, .bcc-h, .bcc-slide, .bcc-img, .bcc-add

5. How recommendations are chosen

For each product the app scores every other in-stock product by how well it complements the current one — favouring different-but-related categories, shared tags/title words and a similar price, with a light nudge from how often items are bought and viewed. An AI step then re-orders the shortlist to best “complete the look”. Only active, in-stock products are ever shown.
